# AI SEO by Content Type

## SaaS product pages

- Lead with what it is / who it’s for (definition block).
- Feature → outcome bullets; concrete limits and integrations.
- Visible pricing or link to `/pricing.md` (no JS-only walls).
- FAQ + `Product` / `Organization` schema.
- Customer metrics with sources beat marketing adjectives.

## Blog / thought leadership

- Answer-first H2s matching query language.
- Stats, quotes, author credentials, last-updated date.
- Prefer definitive guides and original research over thin posts.
- `Article` / `BlogPosting` schema.

## Comparison / alternatives pages

- Honest tables; “choose X if / choose Y if”.
- Cover competitors people actually search.
- High AI citation share (~33% for comparison formats).
- Avoid fake “objective” listicles that only crown yourself.

## Documentation

- Task-oriented how-tos; stable URLs; semantic HTML.
- Definition + steps + troubleshooting FAQ.
- Keep gated docs to a minimum for pages you want cited.
- `HowTo` / `FAQPage` where accurate.

## Local / ecom

- Google Merchant Center feeds + Google Business Profile.
- Clear product specs, stock/price on indexable HTML.
- Reviews (`AggregateRating`) when legitimate.
- Local entities consistent across the web (NAP, GBP).

## Shared priorities

1. Extractable structure (see `content-patterns.md`).
2. Authority signals (sources, experts, freshness).
3. Presence where AI looks (third-party + review platforms).
4. Bot access for platforms you want citations from.
